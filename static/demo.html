Precise location (opt-in)

Every visitor already gets an approximate city from their IP — but that's the ISP's gateway, not the person. On a mobile network it's routinely the wrong city (a Jio user in Pune usually resolves to Mumbai). The only way to know where someone actually is, is to ask: the button below triggers the browser's own permission prompt, and the answer is reverse-geocoded to a street and neighbourhood.
